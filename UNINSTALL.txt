Rat Game Public Matchmaking v0.2.0 - rollback / removal

Quit Rat Game normally and confirm Rat Game.exe has exited before changing files.

To revert an update, replace only
BepInEx/plugins/RatGamePublicMatchmaking/RatGamePublicMatchmaking.dll
with your previous DLL from backup, then verify its recorded SHA-256.
Keep backup DLLs outside BepInEx/plugins to avoid duplicate loading.

To disable this mod, move its DLL/plugin folder outside BepInEx/plugins.
Leave the shared loader and other plugins intact.

Preserve BepInEx/config/local.ratgame.publicmatchmaking.cfg and the entire
BepInEx/config/local.ratgame.publicmatchmaking/ directory, including backups.
Removal/downgrade disables moderation enforcement; v0.1 does not enforce v0.2 bans
even when the banlist is retained.

Launch through Steam and check the intended version (or absence of the mod).
No original game files need removal/replacement. Never delete Rat Game_Data.
For complete loader removal, follow BepInEx instructions after considering other
plugins: removing a shared loader disables them too.
